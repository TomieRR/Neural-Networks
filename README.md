# Neural Networks

### Understanding learning through data, models, and experimentation.

This repository will be dedicated to learning, implementing, and exploring **artificial neural networks** using Python. It will connect theoretical foundations with practical experiments to develop an understanding of how neural networks learn from data, recognize patterns, and solve different types of problems.

## Scope

The project will explore the principles of neural computation, including **artificial neurons, activation functions, network architectures, weight initialization, and learning algorithms**. Particular attention will be given to how data passes through a network, how its outputs are evaluated, and how its parameters are adjusted during training.

The work will cover concepts from **supervised and unsupervised learning**, exploring tasks such as classification, regression, clustering, and data representation. Topics will include perceptrons, multilayer networks, backpropagation, competitive learning, and self-organizing maps, alongside broader ideas in neural network design and training.

Experiments will investigate how data preprocessing, architecture choices, learning rates, and other training parameters affect model behavior and performance. Analysis and visualization will help explain convergence, generalization, overfitting, and the limitations of different approaches.

## Learning Goals

- Understand the mathematical and conceptual foundations of artificial neural networks.
- Learn how different network architectures and learning mechanisms work.
- Prepare and normalize data for training and evaluation.
- Implement neural models and examine how their weights change during learning.
- Compare approaches and explore the influence of model and training parameters.
- Evaluate results and understand how well a model generalizes to unseen data.
- Visualize learning behavior and interpret the patterns discovered by a network.

The repository will grow alongside the learning process, serving as a space for experimentation, documenting observations, and building practical experience in **neural networks and machine learning**.
